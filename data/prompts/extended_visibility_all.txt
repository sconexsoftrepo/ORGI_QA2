- Hanging decoration: banners, paper strips, flag chains
- Must be suspended above counter/shelf/open space – not stuck to surface
- Must display Coca-Cola branding

**1021 – Coca-Cola Non-Digital Combo Board**
- Printed board showing food + Coca-Cola product together

**1024 – Coca-Cola Bar Signage**

**1027 – Coca-Cola Branded Table Sticker**

**1028 – Coca-Cola Branded Bar Mat**

**1029 – Coca-Cola Branded Napkin Holder**

- Must show Coca-Cola branding clearly
